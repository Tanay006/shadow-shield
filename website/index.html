Process

How It Works

Three layers of protection — all in real-time.

01

Intercept

Shadow Shield hooks into the page's send button, keyboard shortcuts, and form submissions using document-level capture listeners — firing before the AI platform's own handlers.

02

Scan & Detect

Your text runs through a multi-pass PII engine: 11+ regex patterns, context-aware label detection (e.g. "Name: John Doe"), confidence scoring with keyword proximity boosting, and Luhn checksum for credit cards.

03

Replace & Send

Detected PII items are shown in the approval modal. You pick which ones to mask. Selected items are replaced with format-preserving fake data, and the sanitized text is sent to the AI.
